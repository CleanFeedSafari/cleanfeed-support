CleanFeed brings personal filters, focus profiles and mindful breaks to X in Safari. Choose what stays in your feed, then give yourself time to focus.

Set up CleanFeedRead our privacy policy
01 / CHOOSE

Make your own filters

Hide videos, reposts, replies, blocked keywords and posts outside your allow list. Use a separate keyword list for quoted posts.

02 / FOCUS

A profile for your purpose

Keep different rules for work, news or your own interests. Lock your choices for a timed focus session.

03 / PAUSE

Know when to stop

Set an active-time or viewed-post limit. A gentle break screen lets you pause or start a new session.

Your settings stay with you.

Filtering runs on your device. CleanFeed has no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cloud sync. Your profile rules are stored locally.

Available for Safari on iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The extension supports X’s English and Turkish interface. It does not work inside the X app.

CleanFeed filters posts that X has already loaded. It does not fetch extra posts, block X’s own tracking or guarantee that hidden videos are not downloaded.

CleanFeed · An independent Safari extension. Not affiliated with or endorsed by X.
