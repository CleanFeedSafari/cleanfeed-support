What the app processes

When you grant Safari site access, the extension reads relevant elements of the open X page to apply your filters. These can include post text, quoted text, author handles, recognized post identifiers, media indicators, tab labels and engagement counts. Processing happens on your device. CleanFeed does not send this page content to a developer server.

Accounts, tracking and permissions

CleanFeed has no separate user account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cloud sync. It does not request access to browser history, cookies, your camera, microphone or photo library. It does not implement collection of passwords, authentication tokens or direct messages.

Safari’s website permission is broad enough to allow reading and modifying permitted pages. The extension’s scripts are limited to x.com, www.x.com, twitter.com, www.twitter.com and mobile.twitter.com. It also uses local settings storage and temporary active-tab access. You control site permissions in Safari settings.

X remains a separate service

You sign into X directly in Safari. X and Safari continue handling their own cookies, network requests and data under their own policies. CleanFeed does not prevent X’s tracking or guarantee that hidden content is not downloaded. CleanFeed is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by X.

See X’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Your profiles and preferences persist locally until changed, reset or removed with the app’s storage. With focus lock off, Privacy and help → Delete saved profiles and restore defaults removes your custom profiles and rules and replaces them with defaults. It retains language and display preferences. The native app’s language is managed separately.

Session statistics are temporary. Reset clears the current tab’s statistics; reloading or closing the tab discards its session memory. Profile or filter-rule changes start a new session. The focus-lock end time is persistent and is not removed by closing a tab.

You can revoke access in Safari or delete the app to uninstall the extension. Revoking site access does not itself delete saved settings. These actions do not delete X account data or Safari cookies. Device backup and restore behavior is controlled by Apple and your device settings; CleanFeed does not operate a backup service.

Private Browsing

If you allow the extension in Private Browsing, Safari may make the same saved profiles available there. Rules you add in a private session can remain stored locally. Private Browsing does not make CleanFeed’s saved preferences temporary.

Support messages

If you choose to email support, the publisher receives the email address, message and attachments you send. They are used to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Email providers process delivery and storage. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or sensitive page content.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle requests, follow-up issues and applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can request deletion using the contact above. The publisher cannot remotely retrieve or delete your device-only filter rules; use the local controls described above.

This website and GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed into GitHub. This website adds no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, embedded social feeds or tracking cookies.

GitHub’s handling of hosting data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Opening external links subjects the visit to the destination’s policies. Hosting and email providers may process information in other countries.

You may edit or delete local rules, stop filtering, remove site permission, uninstall the extension, or contact the publisher about information you voluntarily provided.
